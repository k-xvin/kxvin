# Zen at Work

![[IMG_2856.jpeg]]

I picked this book up from a used bookstore, Bell's Books. It came with a bookmark!

I started this book sometime in April 2025 and then I finished the book at the end of September 2026. The author of the book, Les Kaye, was alive when I started the book. He passed away in early September, a little before I finished the book. It's an old book and I knew Kaye was an old guy. So it wasn't unexpected, but it made me a little somber since I really resonated with parts of the book.

This book got me through some low points. I would always feel a little more relaxed and with a broader perspective after reading a few passages or chapters. It's a lot of good thinking material.

I was also always struck by the fact that despite being a "zen master," Kaye was just a human being like all of us, with much of the same emotions and experiences. That makes sense, but sometimes it's easy to forget. I really enjoyed the personal anecdotes that Kaye told throughout this book.
